//!Heading Tags
--> Heading tags are used to define the headings and subheadings of a webpage.
--> HTML provides six heading tags: <h1> to <h6>.
--> All heading tags are paired tags.
--> As we move from <h1> to <h6>, the default font size decreases.

//!Default Browser Font Sizes
h1 - 32px or 2em  (Largest)
h2 - 24px or 1.5em 
h3 - 18.72px or 1.17em 
h4 - 16px or 1em 
h5 - 13.28px or 0.83em 
h6 - 10.72px or 0.67em (Smallest)

//! Note:
--> <h1> is the most important heading on a webpage.
--> <h2> to <h6> are used for subheadings.
