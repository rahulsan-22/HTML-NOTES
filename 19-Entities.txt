//! HTML Entities
--> HTML Entities are special codes used to display reserved characters, special symbols, and characters that cannot be typed directly in HTML.
--> An HTML Entity starts with an ampersand (&) and ends with a semicolon (;).
------------------------------------------------------------
//!Syntax:
&entity_name;

or

&#entity_number;

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Reserved Character Entities

1. Less Than (<)

Entity:
&lt;

Output:
<

------------------------------------------------------------ 

2. Greater Than (>)

Entity:
&gt;

Output:
>

------------------------------------------------------------

3. Ampersand (&)

Entity:
&amp;

Output:
&

------------------------------------------------------------

4. Double Quotes (")

Entity:
&quot;

Output:
"

------------------------------------------------------------

5. Single Quote (')

Entity:
&apos;

Output:
'

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Space Entity

&nbsp;

--> Stands for Non-Breaking Space.
--> It is used to insert an extra space between words.

Example:
Hello&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;World

Output:
Hello     World

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Currency Symbol Entities

&#8377; 

Output:
₹

------------------------------------------------------------

&dollar;

Output:
$

------------------------------------------------------------

&euro;

Output:
€

------------------------------------------------------------

&pound;

Output:
£

------------------------------------------------------------

&yen;

Output:
¥

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Copyright & Trademark Entities

&copy;

Output:
©

------------------------------------------------------------

&reg;

Output:
®

------------------------------------------------------------

&trade;

Output:
™

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Mathematical Symbol Entities

&times;

Output:
×

------------------------------------------------------------

&divide;

Output:
÷

------------------------------------------------------------

&le;

Output:
≤

------------------------------------------------------------

&ge;

Output:
≥

------------------------------------------------------------

//! Example

<p>
5 &lt; 10
</p>

<p>
10 &gt; 5
</p>

<p>
Pavan &amp; Monty
</p>

<p>
Price: &#8377;499
</p>

<p>
Copyright &copy; 2026
</p>

------------------------------------------------------------
